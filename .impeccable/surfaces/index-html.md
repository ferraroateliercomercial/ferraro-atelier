Scope: the homepage of Ferraro Atelier; visitor mode Persuade. The ateliê virtual (#personalizar) and the Oficina (#oficina) share the file and adopt the new tokens, but stay Operate surfaces.

Audience and job: people who like watches without being experts and want a piece nobody else has; they arrive mostly from Instagram. Action: build your own watch in the ateliê virtual and send the project by WhatsApp. Secondary: buy a finished piece (Carbon Edition R$ 1.799, Verde Fumê R$ 1.600). Unresolved: none from the owner; finish review pending.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one living dial seen from above. The viewport is the dial and runs the visitor's own time; pulling back reveals the watch they can build. It refuses the category default of a product photo hero with a headline and a discover button.

OWN-WORLD: Fumé lacquer, deep blue by default and any hue on demand, with sunburst light that follows the pointer; opaline silver for quiet passages; print-white dial printing set in expanded Saira; the red seconds hand is the only accent; lume green only at night. Components are dial parts: minute-track rules, applied baton indices as markers, the date window as counter, sub-dials as cards, the crown as the primary control.

STORY: The visitor sees a working dial on their time, learns each part is chosen, watches colour change freely and the watch come apart, trusts the movements, sees finished pieces, then turns the crown to build theirs and send it by WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport fumé dial; railroad minute track along the screen edges; wings logo printed under 12; headline "Seu relógio, peça por peça." printed across the lower dial; hour and minute hands pivot at the centre on the visitor's time; red seconds hand at 8 steps per second; date window at 3 o'clock showing today; the crown at the right edge is the action, labelled "Monte o seu". No buttons.

FORM: Mostrador vivo, the pick card (rank 1 of my seven) chosen over the assigned index 6; seed key 3ac2addf. Raises kept: ring law, labels orbit the rim and the centre stays clear (accretion disk); one shared clock, motion quantised to 8 beats per second (algorave); select-to-isolate in the anatomy (particle display); scale courage, numerals cropped by the viewport (décollage).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
