# Página inicial (index.html, view #viewHome)

Scope: the homepage of Ferraro Atelier; visitor mode Persuade. The ateliê virtual (#personalizar) and the Oficina (#oficina) share the file and adopt the new tokens, but stay Operate surfaces.

Audience and job: people who like watches without being experts and want a piece nobody else has; they arrive mostly from Instagram. Action: build your own watch in the ateliê virtual and send the project by WhatsApp. Secondary: buy a finished piece (Carbon Edition R$ 1.799, Verde Fumê R$ 1.600). Proof on hand: the working SVG watch, real photos, the movements demo. Constraints: static site with no build, Vercel; owner pinned "no buttons in the first part", a motion-design opening with a watch, the best motion possible.

Memorable moment: the screen is a dial that tells your own time; scrolling pulls back until the dial becomes a whole watch, which then comes apart.

Unresolved: none from the owner. Finish review shipped 2026-10-19; motion smoothing pass the same day (opening and pullback now animate only by transform and opacity of layers prepared in advance).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one living dial seen from above. The viewport is the dial and runs the visitor's own time; pulling back reveals the watch they can build. It refuses the category default of a product photo hero with a headline and a discover button.

OWN-WORLD: Forest palette (owner, 2026-10-19: earthy green, a touch of gold, brown). Moss-green fumé lacquer by default and any hue on demand, with sunburst light that follows the pointer; walnut and leather brown as the second dark ground; linen for quiet passages with walnut ink; ivory dial printing set in expanded Saira; bronze case and crown, gilt indices and hands; the gold seconds hand is the only accent; lume green only at night. Components are dial parts: minute-track rules, applied baton indices as markers, the date window as counter, sub-dials as cards, the crown as the primary control.

STORY: The visitor sees a working dial on their time, learns each part is chosen, watches colour change freely and the watch come apart, trusts the movements, sees finished pieces, then turns the crown to build theirs and send it by WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport fumé dial; railroad minute track along the screen edges; wings logo printed under 12; headline "Seu relógio, peça por peça." printed across the lower dial; hour and minute hands pivot at the centre on the visitor's time; red seconds hand sweeping without stopping (owner, 2026-10-19: the opening must feel fluid, so the 8-step tick was dropped on the homepage dial); date window at 3 o'clock showing today; the crown at the right edge is the action, labelled "Monte o seu". No buttons.

FORM: Mostrador vivo, the pick card (rank 1 of my seven) chosen over the assigned index 6; seed key 3ac2addf. Raises kept: ring law, labels orbit the rim and the centre stays clear (accretion disk); one shared clock, the opening staggered on a 1/8 s grid (the seconds hand itself now sweeps, at the owner's request); select-to-isolate in the anatomy (particle display); scale courage, numerals cropped by the viewport (décollage).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
